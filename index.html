Who Are We?

TRIKA is the new frontier of luxury technology, crafted with the timeless elegance of Tanishq. We are more than a smartwatch — we are a symbol of prestige, precision, and progressive design.

Our vision is to blend the legacy of craftsmanship with the pulse of modern innovation, offering timepieces that are both iconic and intelligent.

Whether in the boardroom, gym, or gala — TRIKA moves with your rhythm and style.

Future Goals

  • Launch Smart Jewelry Ecosystem: Imagine a world where your accessories aren’t just ornamental — they’re intelligent. With AI-powered rings, bracelets, and pendants, TRIKA will create a new definition of wearable elegance.
  • Women’s Safety Innovation: Beauty with a purpose. Our future products will embed discreet safety alerts, giving every woman peace of mind without compromising on style.
  • Gold-as-a-Service (GaaS): Luxury meets flexibility. A subscription-based model lets customers experience new designs, trade-in old ones, and own timeless beauty their way.
  • Global Expansion: From the heart of India to the fashion capitals of the world — we aim to carry our heritage to Milan’s runways, Tokyo’s tech stores, and New York’s high streets.
